# Sidro

**A Windows companion for Warframe that brings inventory scanning, reward prices, and trading tools into one app.**

Sidro reads items from your screen using OCR, checks warframe.market prices, and helps you review and manage your sell listings.

## Features

- **Snip scanning** — Select one item on your screen, review its name, quantity, and price, then create or update its listing.


https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/743564f7-ebe3-4d05-bb0d-745a7271c9b2


- **Inventory scanning** — Scan a batch of inventory items or select a single item from your screen.


https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e4279537-daf5-413c-9b74-fbe4f7376b32


- **Void Fissure scanning** — See reward market values in an overlay and queue selected rewards for review after your run.
- **Trade scanning and monitoring** — Read your offered trade items and synchronize matching sell listings after a detected successful trade.
- **Listing management** — Review prices and quantities, update visibility, and create, update, or delete sell listings.
- **Selling presets** — Choose pricing options such as Most Common, Quicksell, or an offset from the lowest price.
- **Ducat saver** — Identify items better suited to bulk ducat trading using your preferred values.
- **Trade and Recruitment Chat** — Save reusable messages and copy them when needed.
- **Personalization** — Customize hotkeys, appearance, notifications, Sidro visuals, and welcome audio.

## Download and setup

Windows builds will be published on the [Releases page](https://github.com/Hitakumori/Sidro/releases).

1. Download a release ZIP, extract it, and run `Sidro.exe`.
2. Open **Settings** and enter your Warframe in-game name.
3. Connect your warframe.market account through **Login / Token** to use listing management.
4. Review the scanner help and hotkeys before scanning in-game.

Keep Windows HDR disabled while scanning; HDR is unsupported in Sidro 1.0.

Settings and saved account data live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sidro`, so replacing the application folder preserves them.
